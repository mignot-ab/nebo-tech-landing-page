Using the logos

A few rules keep them looking right.

Give it room

Leave clear space around every logo, and avoid placing it on busy photography.

Don't recolour or stretch

Use the files as supplied. Scale them proportionally; never squash, rotate or change their colours.

Icon when space is tight

In app launchers, favicons and social avatars, use the app icon instead of the full logo.
