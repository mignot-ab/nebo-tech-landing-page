Using the marks

A few rules keep the family consistent.

Give it room

Keep clear space around a logo equal to the height of the "N" in NEBO. Don't place it on busy photography.

Don't recolour

Each product owns its hue. Use the white lockup on dark backgrounds rather than changing the icon colour.

Icon alone is fine

Where space is tight — favicons, app launchers, social avatars — the icon can stand without the wordmark.
